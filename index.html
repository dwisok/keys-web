Questions

      
Nothing
in the game.

      
Short answers. KEYED never touches GTA, its servers or your login.

    


    

      

        Can I get banned?+
        
No. KEYED never touches the game, its servers or your login. Nothing runs on your console or PC. The proof reads a page you are already allowed to see, in your own browser.

      

      

        Do I need crypto?+
        
No token, no NFT. You need a Solana wallet to sign, and USDC for entries and prizes. Phantom and Solflare both work.

      

      

        What does KEYED see?+
        
Only what a tournament needs: the game, your playtime, your progress and your crew. Your Social Club name is stored as a hash, never in clear.

      

      

        What happens on November 19?+
        
GTA VI launches. Refresh your passport and it records that its stats were verified against GTA VI. Tournaments can require either game, or both.

      

      

        Who can trigger a payout?+
        
Anyone, once the judges' approvals are in and the dispute window has closed. The program checks both and pays the winners directly from escrow.
